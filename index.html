Privacy Policy

Mushin Machine – Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Mushin Machine publishes philosophy and mindset content (Bushido, Stoicism, Jungian psychology and related topics) on its Facebook Page. This policy explains how information is handled by our publishing application.

2. Information We Collect

Our application only publishes content (images, short videos, captions) to the Mushin Machine Facebook Page. It does not collect, store, or sell personal data of Page visitors, followers, or commenters. We do not use cookies or tracking on this site.

3. Facebook Platform Data

The application uses the Meta Graph API with a Page access token solely to publish posts and read basic Page post status and engagement metrics (such as likes, comments, shares and reach) in aggregate. This data is used only to evaluate our own content.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep only a log of our own published posts and aggregate metrics. If you want any information related to you removed, contact us through a message on the Mushin Machine Facebook Page and we will act on the request promptly. Facebook users can also remove app access at any time in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

6. Children

Our content is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
